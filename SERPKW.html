Why this matters

Your brand keywords should always be protected. If a competitor bids on your brand name, it drives up your CPC and hands them clicks that should be yours — brand terms convert at the highest ROI of any keyword, so losing that traffic is costly.

What to do if you find someone bidding on your name:

  • No agreement yet? Reach out and propose a mutual non-bidding pact — neither side bids on the other's brand terms.
  • Already have a pact but still see their ad? Copy the tracking link from their ad and send them the exact campaign so they can pause it.
